SOLID principles:

[S]ingle Responsibility Principle
[O]pen/Closed Principle
[L]iskov Substitution Principle
[I]nterface Segregation Principle
[D]ependency Inversion Principle

Single Responsibility principles(Separation of concerns)
Open/Close principles(software entities (classes, modules, functions, etc.) should be open for extensions, but closed for modification.)
Liskov Substitution priciples(whenever a parent is expected,child can be sent)
Interface Segregation principles(Client should not be forced to use or override the method which it does not want to use. Create role interfaces)
Depend on abtraction of loose coupling
