SAGA pattern for consistency of data in microservice (Database per service pattern)

SAGA is a sequence of local transactions among multiple microservices modules for a single business transaction
Like ordering a product can have mulple processes underneath like confirming order,reducing count from product inverntory, making payment, triggering a mail to customer on confirmation.
Similarly if a local transaction fails due to some business failure then a rollback saga series needs to be executed.

Two ways in which SAGA can be implemented
1) Choreography
2) Orchestration
